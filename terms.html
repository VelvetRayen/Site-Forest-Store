Store Rules & Guidelines

1. Welcome to Forest Store

By using our website or shop, you agree to follow our simple rules. If you don't agree with anything here, please feel free to leave the site.

2. What We Offer

We provide digital gaming items, top-ups, and codes. We can update prices or change items anytime without prior notice.

3. Your Account

Keep your account password safe. You are fully responsible for anything that happens using your account details.

4. Buying & Paying

All purchases depend on stock availability. We have the right to cancel any suspicious order and refund the money if needed.

5. Getting Your Products

Digital codes or top-ups arrive right after payment. Once a code is shown to you, we cannot take it back unless it is broken or doesn't work.

6. Using Platform Rules

Items you buy must also follow the rules of original game companies (like Steam, PlayStation, or Xbox).

7. What You Cannot Do

No hacking, no fake payments, no spamming support, and no reselling our items without asking us first.

8. Website Design

All logos, text, and styles on Forest Store belong to us. Please do not copy them for other websites.

9. Need Help?

If you have any questions about these rules, drop us a message at

foreststore@gmail.com.

Privacy Policy (Forest Services)

1. Information Protection

We respect your privacy. Any information you provide within the server or store will remain confidential and will not be shared with any third party.

2. Data Usage

The information collected is used only to provide services and improve the user experience within the server and store.

3. No Misuse of Data

Your data will not be used for purposes unrelated to the services provided by Forest Services and Forest Store.

4. Security

We strive to keep user information secure, but users are responsible for protecting their own accounts and credentials.

5. Cookies & Browsing

Our site uses basic cookies to save your settings and make your shopping experience smooth and fast.

6. Age Limit

Our store is meant for users who are 18 years old or older. We don't intentionally collect info from children.

7. Got Questions?

If you want to know more about your privacy or clear your data, contact us anytime at

foreststore@gmail.com.
